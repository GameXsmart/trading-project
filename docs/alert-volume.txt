Alert volume - raw output
=========================

The Phase 11 gate is a rate budget, so this measures the thing the gate is about:
how many alerts a reader would actually have received.

Replayed hour by hour over the real stored history for BTC, ETH and SOL on 1h, with
the default budget (6/hour, 30/day, 4h cooldown per kind and asset, 12h dedup window,
plus a small reserve that only CRITICAL alerts may draw on).

"raised" counts what the rules produced; "delivered" counts what a human would have
been shown. The gap is the whole point of the phase, and it is reported to the reader
as a periodic digest rather than swallowed.

Note the absence: zero directional alerts across 29 days, because no model has earned
a weight and the ensemble never publishes.

Committed as evidence. See docs/PHASES.md, Phase 11, for the analysis.
